Frequently asked questions

Is SplitUp really free?

Yes — 100% free, forever, with no ads and no premium tier. It's fully open source under the MIT License, so you can even inspect exactly how it works.

Where can I download it?

Right now, straight from GitHub Releases (link above). A public Google Play Store listing is being prepared — watch the repo to hear about it first.

Does my data leave my phone?

No, not unless you choose to. Everything is stored encrypted on your device. Cloud sync is entirely optional, per-account, and only syncs your own data with your own sign-in.

Does receipt scanning need internet?

No. Text recognition runs entirely on-device with Google ML Kit — your receipt photos never leave your phone.

How are currencies handled?

Live exchange rates are fetched automatically with a built-in offline fallback, so mixed-currency groups always settle fairly.

Is an iOS version planned?

SplitUp is Android-first today. Star or watch the GitHub repository — platform announcements will be posted there.
