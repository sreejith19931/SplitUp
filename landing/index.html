What's New in v1.2.0
Latest Update
  • • Settled Group Archiving: You can now archive fully settled groups to keep your active groups list clean. Find and manage them anytime under the new "Archived" tab.
  • • Full-Screen Quick Actions Dimming: Tapping the (+) action button on the dashboard now dims the whole screen for better focus.
  • • Clean & Neutral UI: Removed unnecessary check mark symbols across pages and standardized neutral backgrounds across settings sub-pages.
  • • Data & Danger Zone Refinements: Added clean borders and clear confirmation steps for data resets and app security.

Smart Expense Splitting

Split group costs equally or custom per member with automatic balance simplification so you make fewer transfers.

Receipt Scanning

Take a photo of any receipt to extract items and line costs using on-device text recognition.

Live Currency Conversion

Real-time exchange rates for multi-currency group trips so everyone pays in their preferred currency.

100% Offline First

Your financial records live locally on your device with optional real-time cloud backup when connected.
