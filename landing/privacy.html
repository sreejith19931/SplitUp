Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2024 • Last Revised: October 2024

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection of personal data by SplitUp ("we", "us", or "our") when you install, access, or use the SplitUp mobile application for Android or our web services at splitupfin.vercel.app (collectively, the "Services").

SplitUp is built on a local-first privacy model designed to prioritize user autonomy, data isolation, and cryptographic security. We do not sell, rent, license, or commercially exploit personal user data or financial transaction records.

2. Categories of Information We Process

  1. Account Registration Data: When you create an account, we process your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) managed securely via Google Firebase Authentication.
  2. Financial & Transaction Records: Individual personal expenses, group expense records, custom split ratios, settlement records, category budgets, and recurring subscriptions created by you.
  3. Device Permission Data (Processed Locally):
    • Camera Access (`CAMERA`): Used exclusively for real-time, on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) receipt parsing via Google ML Kit. Captured image bitmaps are processed in temporary sandbox memory (`cacheDir`) and are not transmitted to external servers.
    • Contacts Access (`READ_CONTACTS`): Read strictly in-memory on your device to discover which of your phone contacts are registered SplitUp users. Your contact list is never uploaded, stored remotely, or shared with third-party marketers.
    • Notification Access (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`): Parsed strictly locally on-device to extract monetary figures from incoming bank and wallet application alerts. Notification text is processed in memory and is never logged or transmitted off-device.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): To deliver core bill-splitting, group balance calculation, and account synchronization services.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To ensure application security, prevent fraudulent activity, and maintain system performance.
  • User Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For runtime device permissions including camera, contacts, and notification access, which you can revoke at any time in device settings.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-Processors

We restrict data processing to essential enterprise infrastructure providers operating under strict confidentiality and security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages user authentication and encrypted database cloud sync. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Google ML Kit (On-Device Vision): Executes local machine learning text recognition without sending receipt imagery to cloud APIs.

5. Data Security & Storage Encryption

SplitUp employs industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • On-Device Encryption: Sensitive preference keys and local state are encrypted using Android KeyStore MasterKey AES-256-GCM encryption (`EncryptedSharedPreferences`).
  • Network Security: All API communication and Firestore cloud synchronization utilize Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption.

6. User Data Rights & Account Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)

In accordance with global privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA, you retain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Right to Data Portability (GDPR Art. 20): You can export complete copies of your data as encrypted JSON database backups, CSV transactions, or PDF statements directly inside the app.
  • Right to Erasure / Account Deletion (GDPR Art. 17): You can instantly delete your account and wipe all cloud records inside the app under Settings > Data Management & Backups > Delete Account. Alternatively, you can submit an external request on our Web Account Deletion Request Portal.

7. Children's Privacy Notice

SplitUp is intended for general adult audiences and is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children.
