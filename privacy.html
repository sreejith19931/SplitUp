Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

1. Local-First Data Philosophy

SplitUp is built on a privacy-respecting, local-first architecture. Your personal expenses, category budgets, receipts, and group records reside locally on your device. SplitUp does not sell, license, or monetize user data.

2. Device Permission Disclosures

  • Camera Access (`CAMERA`): Used exclusively for real-time on-device OCR receipt recognition via Google ML Kit. Captured photos remain on your device and are processed in local sandbox cache (`cacheDir`).
  • Contacts Access (`READ_CONTACTS`): Processed strictly locally in memory on your device to help you discover friends on SplitUp and add them to expense groups. Your contact list is never uploaded or transmitted off-device.
  • Notification Access (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`): Processed strictly locally on-device to extract monetary figures from bank and wallet app alerts. Notification content is parsed in memory and is never logged or transmitted off-device.

3. Cloud Synchronization & Data Safety

If you create an account, optional cloud sync uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Cloud data is isolated to authenticated account members.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Data Export (GDPR Art. 20): You can export full JSON backups, CSV reports, or PDF statements at any time inside the app.
  • Account & Data Erasure (GDPR Art. 17): You can instantly delete your account and clear all cloud records inside the app under Settings > Data Management & Backups > Delete Account, or submit a request on our Account Deletion Request Portal.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at GitHub Support.
